Care tips

Keep your carpets and furniture looking new

Four simple habits that make your carpet and upholstery last longer.

1

Vacuum often

Vacuuming is the most important step. Traffic areas and stairs need it at minimum, and a couple of times a month is not enough to stop texture loss.

2

Clean every 12 to 18 months

High-traffic carpets, rugs and furniture should be professionally cleaned every 12 to 18 months. Stairs, halls, pet areas and TV rooms may need more.

3

Mind your footwear

Wear indoor shoes without black rubber or tanned leather soles. Perspiration and body oils from socks and bare feet trap soil that vacuuming cannot remove.

4

Hot water extraction

Also called steam cleaning, it is the method that removes body oils and soil. Used regularly, it helps prevent texture loss for years.
